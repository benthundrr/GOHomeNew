The rules it works under

It won't make anything up.

On your data

Thundrr AI identifies you from your own login rather than anything the app sends it, so it can only ever answer about your account. Benchmarks are anonymous aggregates — no other creator is ever named, and nobody can ask it about you.

On making things up

If a metric hasn't been imported yet it says so rather than estimating, and it never quotes earnings for a specific property. Strategy advice is open; numbers are only ever your own.
